Strawberry Avocado Toast

Sweet+Creamy+Fruity ~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · fruitfood

Get ready for a yummy surprise with Strawberry Avocado Toast! It sounds like a funny combo, but the creamy avocado and sweet strawberries make a super delicious and healthy snack. You'll love how fresh and tasty it is!

Ingredients

  • 1 slice whole wheat toast
  • 1/4 ripe avocado, mashed
  • 3-4 fresh strawberries, sliced
  • Pinch of salt
  • Drizzle of honey (optional)

Steps

  1. Toast bread until golden.
  2. Mash avocado and spread on toast.
  3. Arrange strawberry slices on top.
  4. Sprinkle with salt and honey.
