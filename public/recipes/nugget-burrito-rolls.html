Nugget Burrito Rolls

Cheesy+Savory+Fun! ~350 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savoryfood

Do you love chicken nuggets AND burritos? Well, get ready for a super fun mash-up! These Nugget Burrito Rolls are a delicious and easy snack that combines all your favorites into one yummy bite. It might sound a little silly, but trust us, it's so good!

Ingredients

  • 6 frozen chicken nuggets
  • 2 small flour tortillas
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp ranch dressing

Steps

  1. Cook nuggets according to package.
  2. Warm tortillas in microwave for 15 seconds.
  3. Place 3 nuggets on each tortilla.
  4. Top with cheese, ketchup, and ranch.
  5. Roll up tightly like a burrito.
