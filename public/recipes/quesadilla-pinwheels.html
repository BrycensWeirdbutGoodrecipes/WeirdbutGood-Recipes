Quesadilla Pinwheels

Cheesy+Savory+Fun!~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savorysnack

These Quesadilla Pinwheels are a super fun and yummy way to eat your favorite cheesy snack! We're rolling up tortillas with cheese and then slicing them into spirals, like little edible wheels. It's a silly and tasty twist on a classic!

Ingredients

  • 2 flour tortillas (8-inch)
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tbsp salsa (optional)
  • 1 tbsp sour cream (optional)

Steps

  1. Lay tortillas flat.
  2. Sprinkle cheese evenly on tortillas.
  3. Roll tortillas up tightly.
  4. Slice each roll into 6-8 pinwheels.
  5. Serve with salsa and sour cream for dipping.
