Caramel Apple Float

Sweet + creamy + apple ~350 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · candydrink

This is like a super-duper fun apple pie in a cup, but even better because it has ice cream and caramel! It's a wacky drink that tastes like a yummy dessert, perfect for a special treat. Get ready for a taste adventure!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup apple juice
  • 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tablespoons caramel sauce
  • Whipped cream for topping
  • 1 maraschino cherry

Steps

  1. Pour apple juice into a tall glass.
  2. Add two scoops of vanilla ice cream.
  3. Drizzle generously with caramel sauce.
  4. Top with a swirl of whipped cream.
  5. Place a cherry on top.
