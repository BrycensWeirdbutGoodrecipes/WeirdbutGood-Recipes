Hibiscus Lime Cooler

Floral+Tangy+Sweet ~70 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · mocktaildrink

This super pretty pink drink is like a magic potion that's both sweet and a little bit tangy! It's weird-but-good because it tastes like a flower garden mixed with sunshine, making your taste buds dance with joy.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons dried hibiscus flowers
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon honey or agave
  • 1/2 cup ice

Steps

  1. Boil water, then add hibiscus flowers.
  2. Steep 5 minutes, then strain out flowers.
  3. Stir in lime juice and honey.
  4. Pour over ice and enjoy!
