Midnight Cookies

Sweet+Salty+Chewy ~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · late-nightsnack

Ever had a cookie that's a little bit salty and a little bit sweet, all at the same time? These Midnight Cookies are super easy to make for a late-night snack, with a secret ingredient that makes them extra yummy and a little bit surprising! They're soft, chewy, and perfect for when you need a quick treat.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup butter, softened
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon chocolate chips
  • 1 pinch sea salt flakes

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cream butter and sugar until fluffy.
  3. Mix in egg yolk.
  4. Stir in flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Fold in chocolate chips.
  6. Drop spoonfuls onto a baking sheet.
  7. Sprinkle with sea salt flakes.
  8. Bake 8-10 minutes until edges are golden.
