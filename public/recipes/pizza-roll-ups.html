Pizza Roll-Ups

Cheesy+Tomatoey+Savory~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savorysnack

Get ready for a super fun and yummy snack that's like a tiny pizza you can roll up! We're taking all the best pizza flavors and making them into a tasty bite-sized treat. It's weird because it's not a normal pizza, but it's good because it's so easy to eat and so delicious!

Ingredients

  • 4 slices sandwich bread
  • 2 tbsp pizza sauce
  • 1/4 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 4 slices pepperoni
  • 1 tbsp melted butter

Steps

  1. Flatten bread with a rolling pin.
  2. Spread sauce on each bread slice.
  3. Sprinkle cheese and add pepperoni.
  4. Roll up each slice tightly.
  5. Brush rolls with melted butter.
  6. Air fry at 350°F for 4-5 mins.
