Avocado Toast Bites

Creamy+Crunchy+Salty~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savorysnack

Get ready for a super yummy and easy snack that’s perfect for little hands! These avocado toast bites are like tiny green sandwiches on crunchy bread, packed with healthy goodness. It might sound a little different, but the creamy avocado on crispy toast with a sprinkle of salt is a surprisingly delicious combo!

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 slices whole wheat bread
  • Pinch of salt
  • Pinch of black pepper
  • Squeeze of lemon juice (optional)

Steps

  1. Toast bread until golden.
  2. Mash avocado in a bowl.
  3. Stir in salt, pepper, and lemon.
  4. Spread avocado on toast.
  5. Cut toast into small squares.
