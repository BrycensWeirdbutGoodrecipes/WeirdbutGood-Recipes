Apple Cinnamon Crepes

Sweet+Warm+Apple~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · fruitfood

Imagine a super thin pancake, but instead of just butter, it's filled with yummy, warm apples and a sprinkle of cinnamon! It's like a warm apple pie hug wrapped in a soft blanket, perfect for breakfast or a sweet treat. It might sound fancy, but it's really easy to make and tastes amazing!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 apples, diced
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp butter

Steps

  1. Whisk flour, eggs, and milk for crepe batter.
  2. Cook thin crepes in a pan.
  3. Sauté apples, cinnamon, and sugar.
  4. Fill crepes with apple mix and fold.
