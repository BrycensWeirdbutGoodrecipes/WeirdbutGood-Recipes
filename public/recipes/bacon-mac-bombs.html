Bacon Mac Bombs

Cheesy+Crispy+Savory~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savoryfood

Get ready for a cheesy, crispy explosion of flavor! These Bacon Mac Bombs are super fun little bites of macaroni and cheese wrapped in bacon, making them perfectly weird and wonderfully delicious for everyone. It's like your favorite comfort food got a crunchy, savory upgrade!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked macaroni & cheese
  • 6 slices bacon
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp oil

Steps

  1. Scoop mac & cheese into 6 small balls.
  2. Wrap each ball with a bacon slice.
  3. Roll in breadcrumbs and cheddar.
  4. Fry in oil until bacon is crispy.
