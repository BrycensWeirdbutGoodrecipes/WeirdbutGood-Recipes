Mango Chili Lassi

Sweet+Creamy+Spicy~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · fruitdrink

This super yummy drink is like a creamy mango smoothie, but with a secret spicy kick! It's a little bit sweet, a little bit tangy, and a tiny bit tingly, making it a fun and surprising treat that your taste buds will love. It's weird-but-good because the chili makes the sweet mango even sweeter!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chopped mango
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • Pinch of chili powder

Steps

  1. Put mango, yogurt, milk, honey in blender.
  2. Add a tiny pinch of chili powder.
  3. Blend until super smooth and creamy.
  4. Pour into a glass and enjoy!
