Cottage Cheese Crisps

Cheesy+Crunchy+Savory ~40 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · low-calsnack

Get ready for a super crunchy, cheesy snack that's easy to make! These crisps are like little toasted cheese bites, but they start with cottage cheese, which sounds a bit funny, but trust us, they're yummy! They get all crispy and golden in the oven, perfect for a quick and healthy treat.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup cottage cheese
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Pinch of black pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Mix cottage cheese with garlic powder and pepper.
  3. Spoon small dollops onto parchment-lined tray.
  4. Flatten each dollop into a thin circle.
  5. Bake for 20-25 minutes until golden and crisp.
