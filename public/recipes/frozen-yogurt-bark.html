Frozen Yogurt Bark

Sweet+Tangy+Fruity ~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · frozensnack

This super cool treat is like a yummy, crunchy, and creamy snack all in one! It's a bit like a chocolate bar, but made with tangy yogurt and your favorite fruity toppings. It might sound a little strange to freeze yogurt flat, but it tastes so good you'll want to make it again and again!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup vanilla yogurt
  • 1/4 cup mixed berries
  • 1 tablespoon mini chocolate chips
  • 1 teaspoon honey

Steps

  1. Line a tray with parchment paper.
  2. Spread yogurt thinly on the paper.
  3. Sprinkle berries and chocolate chips.
  4. Drizzle with honey.
  5. Freeze for 2 hours until firm.
  6. Break into pieces and enjoy!
