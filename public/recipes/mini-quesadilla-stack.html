Mini Quesadilla Stack

Cheesy++ Savory+ ~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savorysnack

Get ready for a cheesy, mini adventure! These tiny quesadillas are stacked high for a fun snack that's easy to eat. It's like a delicious, cheesy tower you can gobble up!

Ingredients

  • 4 small flour tortillas
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup salsa (optional)

Steps

  1. Melt butter in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Place a tortilla in the pan.
  3. Sprinkle cheese on half the tortilla.
  4. Fold tortilla in half, cook until golden.
  5. Cut into small triangles and stack.
