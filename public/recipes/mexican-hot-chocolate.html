Mexican Hot Chocolate ️

Spicy Sweet Warm +++ ~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotdrink

This isn't your grandma's hot chocolate! We're adding a little secret spice to make it warm and tingly, like a cozy hug with a tiny kick. It sounds a little wacky, but trust us, it's super yummy and fun to try something new!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • tiny pinch of chili powder (optional)

Steps

  1. Pour milk into a small pot.
  2. Add cocoa, sugar, cinnamon, and chili.
  3. Whisk well while heating on low.
  4. Heat until steamy, not boiling.
  5. Pour into a mug and enjoy!
