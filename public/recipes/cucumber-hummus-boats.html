Cucumber Hummus Boats

Fresh + Creamy + Crunchy ~80 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · low-calsnack

These aren't your typical boats, but they're super yummy! We're scooping creamy hummus into cool cucumber halves, making a crunchy and delicious snack that's perfect for a healthy treat. It's like a tiny, tasty adventure for your mouth!

Ingredients

  • 1 large cucumber
  • 1/2 cup hummus
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
  • Pinch of paprika

Steps

  1. Wash and slice cucumber in half lengthwise.
  2. Scoop out seeds with a small spoon.
  3. Fill cucumber halves with hummus.
  4. Sprinkle with parsley and paprika.
