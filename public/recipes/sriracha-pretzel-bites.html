Sriracha Pretzel Bites

Spicy+Crunchy+Salty~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotsnack

These Sriracha Pretzel Bites are a super fun and spicy snack that will make your taste buds tingle! It's a little bit weird to put hot sauce on pretzels, but trust us, it's a delicious and crunchy adventure you'll love. Get ready for a flavorful kick!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mini pretzel twists
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha sauce
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp onion powder

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C).
  2. Mix butter and Sriracha in a bowl.
  3. Add pretzels, garlic, and onion powder; toss.
  4. Spread pretzels on a baking sheet.
  5. Bake for 10 minutes until crispy.
