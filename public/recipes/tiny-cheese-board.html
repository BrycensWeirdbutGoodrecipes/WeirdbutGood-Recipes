Tiny Cheese Board

Cheesy+Crunchy+Sweet ~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · pescetarian vegetariansnack

Ever wanted a whole cheese board just for YOU? Now you can! This tiny board has yummy cheese, crunchy crackers, and sweet grapes, all in a super small size that's easy to make and fun to eat. It's weird because it's so small, but good because it's all your favorite snacks on one plate!

Ingredients

  • 1 small slice cheddar cheese
  • 3 round butter crackers
  • 5 red grapes
  • 1 small dollop cream cheese
  • 1 tiny pinch everything bagel seasoning

Steps

  1. Cut cheese into small shapes.
  2. Arrange cheese and crackers on a plate.
  3. Add grapes to the plate.
  4. Spoon cream cheese onto a cracker.
  5. Sprinkle seasoning on cream cheese.
