Salmon Cream Toast

Creamy+Savory+Fishy~280 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · pescetariansnack

Get ready for a super yummy and easy snack! This Salmon Cream Toast is like a fancy fish sandwich but on crunchy toast, topped with creamy goodness and yummy salmon. It's a bit different, but trust us, it's so good and fun to eat!

Ingredients

  • 2 slices whole wheat bread
  • 2 tablespoons cream cheese
  • 1/4 cup canned salmon, drained
  • 1 pinch black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon fresh dill, chopped

Steps

  1. Toast bread until golden and crisp.
  2. Mix cream cheese, salmon, pepper, and dill.
  3. Spread salmon mixture evenly on toast.
  4. Cut toast in half and serve immediately.
