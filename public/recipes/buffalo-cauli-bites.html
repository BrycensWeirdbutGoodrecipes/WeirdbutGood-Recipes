Buffalo Cauli Bites ️

Spicy+Crunchy+Yummy~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotsnack

Get ready for a veggie adventure with these Buffalo Cauli Bites! They're like tiny, spicy chicken wings but made from cauliflower, which sounds super weird but tastes surprisingly yummy. It's a fun way to eat your veggies with a kick!

Ingredients

  • 1 head cauliflower, chopped
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 cup buffalo sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Mix flour and water for batter.
  3. Dip cauliflower in batter, then bake 15 min.
  4. Toss with buffalo sauce, bake 10 more min.
