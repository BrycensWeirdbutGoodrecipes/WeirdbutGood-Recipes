Lemon Zinger Soda

Sour+++ Fizzy+ Sweet+ ~80 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · sourdrink

Get ready for a super sour, fizzy, and fun drink that will make your taste buds tingle! This Lemon Zinger Soda is like a lemonade party in your mouth, but with an extra kick that's weirdly wonderful and will definitely wake you up!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 cup cold water
  • 1/2 cup club soda
  • Lemon slices for garnish

Steps

  1. Squeeze fresh lemon juice into a glass.
  2. Stir in sugar until it dissolves.
  3. Add cold water and mix well.
  4. Top with club soda for fizz.
  5. Garnish with a lemon slice.
