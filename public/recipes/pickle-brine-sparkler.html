Pickle Brine Sparkler

Salty+Sour+Sweet+Bubbly~30 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savorydrink

This zesty drink takes pickle juice and makes it bubbly and fun! It's a surprising mix of sour, salty, and sweet that might sound strange, but tastes super refreshing and yummy, especially on a hot day. Get ready for a pickle party in your mouth!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup pickle juice
  • 1/2 cup sparkling water
  • 1 teaspoon honey or maple syrup
  • 1 pickle spear (for garnish)
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Fill a glass with ice cubes.
  2. Pour in the pickle juice.
  3. Add the sparkling water.
  4. Stir in the honey or maple syrup.
  5. Garnish with a pickle spear.
