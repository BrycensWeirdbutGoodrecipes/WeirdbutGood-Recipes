Strawberry Basil Lassi

Sweet+Fruity+Herbal~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · vegetariandrink

This yummy drink is like a super-duper thick smoothie, but with a surprising twist! We're blending sweet strawberries with a secret herb called basil to make a frothy, fruity, and fresh treat that's a little bit weird but a whole lot delicious. It's like a strawberry shake that grew up and got fancy!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup frozen strawberries
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 2-3 fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Put all ingredients in a blender.
  2. Blend until super smooth and creamy.
  3. Pour into a fun glass.
  4. Enjoy your yummy lassi!
