One-Handed Burrito

Cheesy+Savory+Warm~350 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · gamerfood

This burrito is super cool because you can eat it with just one hand, leaving your other hand free for important stuff like holding your game controller! It's packed with yummy stuff that makes it a perfect snack for when you're busy leveling up or watching your favorite show. No mess, all fun!

Ingredients

  • 1 large flour tortilla
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons cooked rice
  • 1 tablespoon refried beans
  • 1 tablespoon salsa

Steps

  1. Lay tortilla flat.
  2. Spread beans, rice, cheese, and salsa.
  3. Fold sides in, then roll up tightly.
  4. Microwave for 20-30 seconds.
