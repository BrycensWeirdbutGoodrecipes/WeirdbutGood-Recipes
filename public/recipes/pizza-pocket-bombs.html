Pizza Pocket Bombs

Cheesy+Savory+Warm~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savoryfood

Get ready for a cheesy, saucy explosion in every bite! These Pizza Pocket Bombs are like tiny pizzas you can hold, perfect for a fun snack or a quick lunch. They're weird because they're not flat, but super good because all the yummy pizza goodness is packed inside!

Ingredients

  • 1 can (8 oz) crescent roll dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup mini pepperoni
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Unroll crescent dough; separate into triangles.
  3. Spoon sauce, cheese, pepperoni onto dough.
  4. Roll up dough, pinching edges to seal.
  5. Brush with olive oil. Bake 10-12 mins.
