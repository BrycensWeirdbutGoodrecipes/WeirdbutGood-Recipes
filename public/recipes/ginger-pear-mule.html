Ginger Pear Mule

Sweet+Fizzy+Zingy~90 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · mocktaildrink

This fizzy, fruity drink is like a party in a glass! Sweet pear and a little zing from ginger make a super cool combination that might sound a little weird, but tastes really yummy and refreshing. It's like a grown-up drink, but made just for you!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup pear nectar
  • 1/4 cup ginger ale
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 2-3 thin pear slices
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Fill a glass with ice.
  2. Pour in pear nectar and lime juice.
  3. Top with ginger ale.
  4. Stir gently.
  5. Garnish with pear slices.
