Matcha Coconut Latte

Sweet+Creamy+Earthy ~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · vegetariandrink

This yummy drink is like a creamy green cloud with a hint of tropical fun! It's a special treat because it mixes the earthy taste of matcha tea with sweet coconut milk, making a super cool and tasty drink that feels like a mini vacation for your taste buds.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp matcha powder
  • 2 tbsp hot water
  • 1 cup coconut milk (canned, full-fat)
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Whisk matcha and hot water until smooth.
  2. Mix coconut milk and maple syrup.
  3. Fill a glass with ice cubes.
  4. Pour coconut mix over ice, then matcha.
