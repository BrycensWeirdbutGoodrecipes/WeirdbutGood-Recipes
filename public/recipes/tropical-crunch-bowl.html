Tropical Crunch Bowl

Sweet+Crunchy+Tropical~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · fruitfood

Get ready for a taste adventure with our Tropical Crunch Bowl! It's a super colorful mix of yummy fruits and a secret crunchy topping that makes every bite exciting. You might think fruit and crunch is a bit odd, but trust us, it's a delicious surprise your taste buds will love!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup chopped mango
  • 1/2 cup chopped pineapple
  • 1/4 cup shredded coconut
  • 1/4 cup granola
  • 1/4 cup yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon honey

Steps

  1. Chop mango and pineapple.
  2. Mix fruit and yogurt in a bowl.
  3. Sprinkle with coconut and granola.
  4. Drizzle with honey.
