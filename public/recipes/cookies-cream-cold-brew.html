Cookies & Cream Cold Brew

Sweet + Creamy + Crunchy ~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · coffeedrink

Imagine your favorite cookies and cream ice cream, but in a yummy, chilly coffee drink! It's a little bit coffee, a little bit sweet, and a whole lot of fun with crunchy cookie bits. It might sound funny to mix cookies with coffee, but trust us, it's super delicious!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cold brew coffee
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons chocolate syrup
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 crushed chocolate sandwich cookies
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Pour cold brew, milk, syrup, vanilla in glass.
  2. Stir well to mix everything.
  3. Add ice cubes to the drink.
  4. Sprinkle crushed cookies on top.
