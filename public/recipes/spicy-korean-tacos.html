Spicy Korean Tacos ️

Sweet + Spicy + Crunchy ~450 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotfood

These aren't your average tacos! We're mixing yummy Korean BBQ flavors with a little kick in a crunchy taco shell. It's a super fun and tasty way to try something new, even if it sounds a little wacky!

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp sriracha (optional)
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • 8 hard taco shells
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1/2 cup shredded carrots

Steps

  1. Brown beef in a pan, drain fat.
  2. Stir in soy sauce, brown sugar, sriracha, ginger.
  3. Cook until sauce thickens.
  4. Fill taco shells with beef, lettuce, and carrots.
