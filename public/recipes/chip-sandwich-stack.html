Chip Sandwich Stack

Crunchy + Salty + Fun ~350 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · junkfood

Ever wanted a sandwich but also a snack? This is it! We're stacking up crispy chips between soft bread for a crunchy, yummy, and super easy treat. It might sound a little silly, but trust us, it's weird-good!

Ingredients

  • 2 slices white bread
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cup potato chips (any flavor!)
  • 1 slice American cheese

Steps

  1. Spread mayonnaise on one bread slice.
  2. Layer chips on the mayo side.
  3. Top with cheese slice.
  4. Place second bread slice on top.
  5. Enjoy your crunchy sandwich!
