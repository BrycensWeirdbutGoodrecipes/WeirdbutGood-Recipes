Watermelon Pizza

Fruity+Sweet+Cool ~120 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · freshsnack

Get ready for a super cool and yummy treat that looks like pizza but tastes like summer! We're making a "Watermelon Pizza" with juicy watermelon as our crust and all sorts of colorful fruits and toppings. It's a weird combo that's surprisingly delicious and super fun to eat!

Ingredients

  • 1 small watermelon, sliced into rounds
  • 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup mixed berries
  • 1 kiwi, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon shredded coconut
  • 1 tablespoon mini chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Lay watermelon slices flat on a plate.
  2. Spread yogurt thinly over each slice.
  3. Decorate with berries, kiwi, and coconut.
  4. Sprinkle with mini chocolate chips.
  5. Slice like a pizza and enjoy!
