Ocean Green Juice

Sweet, tropical, refreshing +++ ~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · pescetariandrink

Dive into an underwater adventure with this super cool Ocean Green Juice! It looks like something a mermaid would drink, and even though it's green, it tastes like a refreshing splash of fruit. It's a fun way to get your vitamins and feel like you're swimming in the ocean!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pineapple chunks
  • 1/2 green apple, cored
  • 1/2 banana, peeled
  • 1/4 cup baby spinach
  • 1/2 cup coconut water

Steps

  1. Put all ingredients in a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. Pour into a fun cup.
  4. Enjoy your ocean drink!
