Apple Nachos

Sweet + Crunchy + Fun ~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · sweetsnack

What's better than nachos? Apple nachos! This fun and healthy snack takes crisp apple slices and turns them into a sweet treat with yummy toppings. It's a silly way to eat fruit, but so delicious!

Ingredients

  • 1 large apple, sliced
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp mini chocolate chips
  • 1 tbsp chopped peanuts
  • 1 tsp honey

Steps

  1. Slice apple into thin rounds or wedges.
  2. Arrange apple slices on a plate.
  3. Drizzle with peanut butter and honey.
  4. Sprinkle with chocolate chips and peanuts.
