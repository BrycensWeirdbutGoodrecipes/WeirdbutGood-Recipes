Bell Pepper Crunch Cups ️

Crunchy+Creamy+Savory~70 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · low-calsnack

These Bell Pepper Crunch Cups are like tiny edible bowls filled with yummy, crispy goodness! It might sound a little silly to eat a pepper like a cup, but the sweet crunch of the pepper with the creamy filling is a super fun and healthy snack you'll love. It's a colorful treat that makes snack time exciting!

Ingredients

  • 1 small bell pepper (any color)
  • 2 tablespoons cream cheese (light)
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped cucumber
  • 1 teaspoon everything bagel seasoning
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Wash and cut bell pepper in half. Remove seeds.
  2. Mix cream cheese, cucumber, seasoning, and salt.
  3. Spoon mixture into bell pepper halves.
  4. Enjoy your crunchy cups!
