Berry Pancake Tacos

Sweet+Fruity+Fluffy~350 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · sweetfood

Get ready for a super fun breakfast that's like a party in your mouth! We're taking fluffy pancakes and turning them into yummy tacos, then stuffing them with berries. It's a silly, sweet way to start your day that you'll love!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pancake mix
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 cup mixed berries
  • 1/4 cup whipped cream
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup

Steps

  1. Mix pancake mix and milk in a bowl.
  2. Cook small pancakes (about 4 inches).
  3. Fold pancakes in half like a taco.
  4. Fill with berries, whipped cream, and syrup.
