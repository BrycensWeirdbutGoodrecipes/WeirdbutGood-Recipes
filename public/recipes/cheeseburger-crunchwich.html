Cheeseburger Crunchwich

Savory+Crunchy+Cheesy~450 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savoryfood

Get ready for the Cheeseburger Crunchwich, a super fun way to eat your favorite burger flavors! It's weird because we're putting potato chips right inside, but that salty crunch with the cheesy beef is totally delicious. It's like a party in your mouth!

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 lb ground beef
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 slices white bread
  • 1 tbsp ketchup
  • 1/4 cup potato chips

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Cook beef until browned; drain fat.
  3. Stir in cheese and ketchup.
  4. Spread beef mix on one bread slice.
  5. Top with potato chips and other bread slice.
  6. Grill sandwich until golden brown.
