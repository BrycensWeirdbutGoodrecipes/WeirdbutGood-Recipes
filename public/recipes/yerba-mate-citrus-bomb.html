Yerba Mate Citrus Bomb

Citrusy+Sweet+Bubbly+ ~50 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · energydrink

Get ready to blast off with this super cool, super yummy drink! It's like lemonade but with a secret ingredient that gives you a fun burst of energy, perfect for playing all day. It's a little bit fizzy, a little bit sweet, and totally refreshing!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup brewed yerba mate, chilled
  • 1/2 cup orange juice
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup sparkling water
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Brew yerba mate and chill it.
  2. Mix mate, orange juice, lemon juice, and syrup.
  3. Pour over ice in a tall glass.
  4. Top with sparkling water and stir gently.
