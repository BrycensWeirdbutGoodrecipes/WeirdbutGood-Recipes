Watermelon Jalapeño

Sweet + Spicy + Refreshing ~75 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · fruitdrink

This super-refreshing drink combines sweet watermelon with a tiny zing of jalapeño for a surprising taste adventure! It's like a sweet summer day with a playful little tickle on your tongue. Get ready for a delicious and exciting treat!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cubed watermelon
  • 1/4 inch slice jalapeño (remove seeds)
  • 1/2 cup cold water
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)

Steps

  1. Put watermelon and jalapeño in blender.
  2. Add water, lime juice, and sugar.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Pour through a strainer into a glass.
