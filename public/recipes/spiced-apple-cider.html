Spiced Apple Cider

Sweet + warm + apple~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotdrink

Warm up with a cozy cup of Spiced Apple Cider! It's like a hug in a mug, tasting sweet and a little bit like fall leaves. You'll love sipping this yummy drink, especially when it's chilly outside.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups apple juice
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 whole cloves
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg

Steps

  1. Pour apple juice into a pot.
  2. Add brown sugar, cinnamon stick, and cloves.
  3. Stir in nutmeg.
  4. Heat over medium until warm.
  5. Carefully remove spices before serving.
