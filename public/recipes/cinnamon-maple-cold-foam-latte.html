Cinnamon Maple Cold Foam Latte

Sweet+Maple+Cinnamon~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · coffeedrink

Imagine a yummy coffee drink that feels like a cozy autumn hug! This latte has a special foamy topping that tastes like sweet maple syrup and warm cinnamon, making your coffee extra delicious and fun. It's like a dessert and a drink all in one!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cold brew coffee
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon

Steps

  1. Brew 1 cup cold brew coffee.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk cream, syrup, cinnamon.
  3. Whisk until foamy, about 1-2 minutes.
  4. Pour coffee into a glass.
  5. Top with the cinnamon maple foam.
