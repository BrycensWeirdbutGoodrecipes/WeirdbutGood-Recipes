Cucumber Salsa Boats

Crunchy+Fresh+Tangy~30 cal estimate
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savorysnack

Get ready for a crunchy and refreshing snack that’s super fun to eat! These cucumber boats are filled with a zesty salsa, making a yummy and healthy treat that's weird-but-good because it's like a mini salad in a boat!

Ingredients

  • 1 large cucumber
  • 1/2 cup chopped tomato
  • 1/4 cup chopped red onion
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Cut cucumber in half lengthwise.
  2. Scoop out seeds to make "boats."
  3. Mix tomato, onion, lime juice, and salt.
  4. Spoon salsa into cucumber boats.
