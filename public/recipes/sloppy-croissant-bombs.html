Sloppy Croissant Bombs

Savory+Cheesy+Messy~350 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savoryfood

Get ready for a messy, cheesy, and super fun snack! These Sloppy Croissant Bombs are like a delicious hug of savory meat and flaky croissant, all in one bite. It's weird because croissants are usually fancy, but here they're making a yummy, gooey party in your mouth!

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 cup sloppy joe sauce
  • 1 (8 oz) can crescent rolls
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Brown beef in oil, drain.
  2. Stir in sloppy joe sauce.
  3. Unroll crescent dough, separate.
  4. Spoon meat, sprinkle cheese on each.
  5. Roll up each crescent.
  6. Bake at 375°F for 12-15 min.
