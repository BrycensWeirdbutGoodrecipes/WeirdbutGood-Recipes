Cookies and Cream Shake

Sweet + Creamy + Crunchy ~350 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · creamydrink

Get ready for a super yummy Cookies and Cream Shake! It's like your favorite ice cream in a drink, with crunchy cookie bits mixed in. It might sound a little silly to drink cookies, but trust us, it's a delicious treat!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup vanilla ice cream
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 4 chocolate sandwich cookies
  • 1 tablespoon chocolate syrup

Steps

  1. Break cookies into pieces.
  2. Add all ingredients to a blender.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Pour into a tall glass.
