Flapjack Pizzas

Sweet+Fruity+Fun ~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · sweetfood

Flapjack Pizzas are a super fun way to eat your favorite breakfast treat for dessert! We're taking yummy pancakes and turning them into tiny "pizzas" with sweet toppings. It's weird because it's not real pizza, but it's good because it's delicious and you get to choose your own toppings!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pancake mix
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/4 cup mini marshmallows
  • 2 tbsp syrup

Steps

  1. Mix pancake mix and milk.
  2. Cook small pancakes on griddle.
  3. Top warm pancakes with chocolate and marshmallows.
  4. Microwave for 15 seconds until melted.
  5. Drizzle with syrup.
