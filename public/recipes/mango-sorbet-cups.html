Mango Sorbet Cups

Sweet+Tangy+Fruity ~120 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · frozensnack

These Mango Sorbet Cups are like a sunshine party in your mouth! They're super cold, sweet, and made with real mangoes, making them a yummy frozen treat that's way better than plain ice cream. You'll love the tropical taste!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen mango chunks
  • 1/4 cup orange juice
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1/2 teaspoon lime juice

Steps

  1. Put frozen mango, orange juice, honey, and lime in blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Spoon into small cups.
  4. Freeze for 30 minutes to firm up.
