Noodle Crunchwrap

Cheesy+Noodley+Crispy~450 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savoryfood

Get ready for a super fun Noodle Crunchwrap! It's like a crispy, cheesy hug for your noodles, all wrapped up in a giant tortilla. You might think noodles in a wrap is a bit wacky, but trust us, it's a yummy adventure for your taste buds!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked ramen noodles
  • 1 large flour tortilla
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons marinara sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Steps

  1. Spread marinara on tortilla center.
  2. Pile noodles and cheese in middle.
  3. Fold tortilla edges to make a square.
  4. Cook in oil until golden and crispy.
