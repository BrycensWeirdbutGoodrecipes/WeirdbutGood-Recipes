Chili Lime Mango Sticks

Sweet+Tangy+Spicy! ~120 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotsnack

Get ready for a super yummy and a little bit wacky snack! These mango sticks are juicy, sweet, and then BAM – a little zingy and spicy kick! It’s like a tropical party in your mouth with a tiny firework show, and you'll love how the flavors dance together.

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe mango, peeled and sliced into sticks
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • Pinch of sugar (optional)

Steps

  1. Cut mango into fun sticks.
  2. Put mango sticks in a bowl.
  3. Sprinkle lime juice over mango.
  4. Add chili powder and salt.
  5. Gently mix everything together.
