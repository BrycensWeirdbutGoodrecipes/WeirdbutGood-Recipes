Frozen Grape Bombs

Sweet + Tart + Icy ~60 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · fruitsnack

Get ready for a super cool, super yummy snack that's secretly just grapes! These frozen grape bombs are like tiny bursts of sweet, icy flavor that will make your taste buds tingle. It's a little weird to freeze grapes, but trust us, it's SO good and refreshing!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups seedless grapes
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons sugar

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the grapes.
  2. Mix lime juice and sugar in a bowl.
  3. Add grapes and toss to coat.
  4. Spread grapes on a baking sheet.
  5. Freeze for at least 2 hours.
