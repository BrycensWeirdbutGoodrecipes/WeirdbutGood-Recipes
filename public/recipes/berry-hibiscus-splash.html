Berry Hibiscus Splash

Fruity + floral + bubbly ~25 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · low-caldrink

This Berry Hibiscus Splash is like a magic potion that tastes super yummy and looks really pretty! It's a little bit tangy from the berries and a little bit flowery from the hibiscus, which might sound weird, but it's a super cool and refreshing drink perfect for a hot day.

Ingredients

  • 1 hibiscus tea bag
  • 1 cup hot water
  • 1/4 cup frozen mixed berries
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1/2 cup cold sparkling water
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Steep tea bag in hot water for 5 minutes.
  2. Remove tea bag, let tea cool.
  3. Mash berries with honey in a glass.
  4. Add cooled tea and ice to glass.
  5. Top with sparkling water and stir.
