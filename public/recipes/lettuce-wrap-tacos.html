Lettuce Wrap Tacos

Fresh + Crunchy + Savory ~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · low-calfood

These tacos are a fun twist on a classic, using crunchy lettuce instead of tortillas! It might sound a little different, but the cool lettuce makes them super refreshing and yummy. It's a great way to eat your veggies and still enjoy taco night!

Ingredients

  • 1 head butter lettuce
  • 1/2 lb ground turkey
  • 1/4 cup salsa
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheese
  • 1/4 cup corn
  • 1/4 cup black beans

Steps

  1. Cook turkey until browned.
  2. Stir in salsa, corn, and black beans.
  3. Spoon turkey mix into lettuce leaves.
  4. Top with shredded cheese.
