Fish + Chip Cups

Salty+Crispy+Cheesy~350 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · pescetarianfood

Get ready for a super fun snack that's like a tiny fish and chips party in a cup! We're taking crunchy chips and yummy fish sticks, then baking them together for a warm, crispy treat. It's a silly way to eat your fish, but so delicious!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup potato chips, crushed
  • 4 frozen fish sticks
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Crush chips and divide into 4 muffin cups.
  3. Place one fish stick in each cup.
  4. Top with cheese. Bake 10-12 minutes.
  5. Mix ketchup and mayo for dipping sauce.
