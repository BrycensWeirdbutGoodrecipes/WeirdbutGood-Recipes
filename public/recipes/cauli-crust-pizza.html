Cauli-Crust Pizza

Cheesy++ Crunchy+ ~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · low-calfood

Get ready for a pizza party with a secret ingredient! This Cauli-Crust Pizza is super yummy and sneaky healthy because the crust is made from cauliflower. It might sound weird, but it tastes amazing and you won't even miss the regular crust!

Ingredients

  • 1 head cauliflower, riced
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/4 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Your favorite toppings (like pepperoni or veggies!)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Mix riced cauliflower, 1/2 cup cheese, and egg.
  3. Press mixture onto a baking sheet in a circle.
  4. Bake crust for 15-20 minutes until golden.
  5. Spread sauce, 1/4 cup cheese, and toppings.
  6. Bake for 5-10 more minutes until cheese melts.
