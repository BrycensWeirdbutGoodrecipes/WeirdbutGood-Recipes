Skinny Sunrise Coffee ️

Sweet+Citrusy+Creamy~100 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · ogdrink

This is a super fun and yummy coffee drink that tastes like a sunny morning! It's a little bit like a fancy orange juice and a little bit like a creamy coffee, all mixed up into something wonderfully weird and delicious. You'll love the sweet and citrusy surprise!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup orange juice
  • 1/4 cup strong cold coffee
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla syrup
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Fill a glass with ice.
  2. Pour in the orange juice.
  3. Gently add the cold coffee.
  4. Top with milk and vanilla syrup.
  5. Stir and enjoy your sunrise!
