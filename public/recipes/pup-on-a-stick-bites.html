Pup-on-a-Stick Bites

Savory+Salty+Fun!~90 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savoryfood

These "Pup-on-a-Stick Bites" are like a mini hot dog on a stick, but instead of the usual corn dog, we're using a yummy pretzel! It's super fun to eat and a little bit salty, a little bit savory. It might seem weird to put a hot dog in a pretzel, but trust us, it's a delicious combo!

Ingredients

  • 1 hot dog
  • 1 pretzel stick
  • 1 tbsp cream cheese
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1 pinch salt

Steps

  1. Slice hot dog into 3 small pieces.
  2. Carefully push pretzel stick through hot dog pieces.
  3. Spread cream cheese on hot dog.
  4. Drizzle mustard on top.
