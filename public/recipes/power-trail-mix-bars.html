Power Trail Mix Bars

Sweet+Nutty+Chewy ~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · energysnack

These yummy Power Trail Mix Bars are super easy to make and pack a punch of energy for all your adventures! They might look a little lumpy, but the mix of crunchy nuts, chewy fruit, and sweet chocolate makes them taste amazing and keeps you going strong. It's a weird-but-good snack for playtime or homework!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup mini chocolate chips
  • 1/4 cup raisins
  • 1/4 cup chopped nuts (like almonds or walnuts)

Steps

  1. Mix oats, peanut butter, and honey in a bowl.
  2. Stir in chocolate chips, raisins, and nuts.
  3. Press mixture into a small pan.
  4. Chill for 30 minutes until firm.
  5. Cut into bars.
