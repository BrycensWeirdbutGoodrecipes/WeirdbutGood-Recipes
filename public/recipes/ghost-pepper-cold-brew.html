Ghost Pepper Cold Brew

Spicy+Sweet+Creamy~50 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · ogdrink

This isn't your average coffee! We're making a super cool, super spicy cold brew that will wake you up and make your taste buds tingle. It's a fun and fiery drink for brave adventurers who love a little kick!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cold water
  • 1/4 cup coffee grounds
  • 1 tiny sliver ghost pepper
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 cup milk

Steps

  1. Mix water and coffee grounds.
  2. Add a tiny piece of ghost pepper.
  3. Chill for 12-24 hours.
  4. Strain out grounds and pepper.
  5. Stir in sugar and milk.
