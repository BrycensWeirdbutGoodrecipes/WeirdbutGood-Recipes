Spiced Pumpkin Latte

Sweet + Spicy + Creamy ~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotdrink

Imagine a warm hug in a mug, tasting like your favorite pumpkin pie! This yummy drink is a little bit spicy, a little bit sweet, and super creamy, making it a weird-but-good treat that will make you feel cozy inside. It's like a pumpkin patch party for your taste buds!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons pumpkin puree
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Whipped cream (optional)

Steps

  1. Mix milk, pumpkin, sugar, spice, vanilla.
  2. Heat gently in a mug or small pot.
  3. Stir until warm and bubbly.
  4. Top with whipped cream if you like.
