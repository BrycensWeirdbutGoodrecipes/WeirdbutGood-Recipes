Tropical Storm Boost

Fruity+Sweet+Fizz~80 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · energydrink

Get ready for a super fun burst of energy with Tropical Storm Boost! This drink is like a sweet, sunny day in a glass, with a surprise fizz that makes your tastebuds tingle. It's a little bit wacky but totally yummy!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup orange juice
  • 1/4 cup pineapple juice
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/2 cup sparkling water

Steps

  1. Pour orange juice into a glass.
  2. Add pineapple and lime juice.
  3. Stir in ground ginger.
  4. Top with sparkling water.
