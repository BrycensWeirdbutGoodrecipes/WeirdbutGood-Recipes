Banana PB Power Wraps

Sweet+Nutty+Soft ~280 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · energysnack

These Banana PB Power Wraps are a super fun and yummy snack that will give you energy to play all day! It might sound a little silly to put peanut butter and bananas in a tortilla, but trust us, it's a delicious and healthy treat. It's like a mini dessert that's good for you!

Ingredients

  • 1 small whole wheat tortilla
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 1 teaspoon honey (optional)
  • 1 pinch cinnamon (optional)

Steps

  1. Lay tortilla flat.
  2. Spread peanut butter evenly on tortilla.
  3. Peel banana and place at one edge.
  4. Drizzle honey and sprinkle cinnamon.
  5. Roll up the tortilla tightly.
  6. Slice into fun pinwheels.
