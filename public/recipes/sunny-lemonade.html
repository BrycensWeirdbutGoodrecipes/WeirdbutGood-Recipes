Sunny Lemonade

Sweet+Sour+Bubbly~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · ogdrink

This isn't just any lemonade; it's Sunny Lemonade, bursting with bright lemon flavor and a secret ingredient that makes it extra special and super yummy! It's sweet, tangy, and a little bit bubbly, making it the perfect drink for a sunny day.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 3 cups cold water
  • 1/4 cup lemon-lime soda
  • Thin lemon slices, for garnish

Steps

  1. Squeeze lemons to get 1 cup juice.
  2. Mix lemon juice and sugar in a pitcher.
  3. Stir in cold water until sugar dissolves.
  4. Gently pour in lemon-lime soda.
  5. Serve over ice with a lemon slice.
