London Fog Latte

Sweet + Creamy + Warm ~120 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotdrink

Imagine a cozy cup of tea that tastes like a sweet dream! This London Fog Latte is a warm, creamy drink that mixes the yummy taste of Earl Grey tea with vanilla and milk. It's like a hug in a mug, perfect for a rainy day.

Ingredients

  • 1 Earl Grey tea bag
  • 1/2 cup hot water
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Steps

  1. Steep tea bag in hot water for 3 minutes.
  2. Warm milk in a small pot.
  3. Stir vanilla and sugar into the tea.
  4. Pour warm milk into the tea.
