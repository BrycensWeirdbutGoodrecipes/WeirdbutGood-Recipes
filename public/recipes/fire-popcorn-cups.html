Fire Popcorn Cups

Spicy+Crunchy+Warm ~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotsnack

Get ready for a fiery snack adventure with these Fire Popcorn Cups! We're taking regular popcorn and adding a secret spicy kick that's super fun and a little bit weird, but in a totally delicious way. It’s a warm, crunchy treat that's perfect for movie night or a brave snack time.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups popped popcorn
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1/4 tsp chili powder
  • 1/8 tsp smoked paprika
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Pop the popcorn and put in a bowl.
  2. Melt butter and pour over popcorn.
  3. Sprinkle chili powder, paprika, and salt.
  4. Toss gently until all coated.
  5. Serve in small cups.
