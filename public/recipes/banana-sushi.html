Banana Sushi

Sweet+Fruity+Crunchy ~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · sweetsnack

Get ready for some silly sushi that's actually a sweet banana treat! We're taking yummy bananas and rolling them in fun toppings, then slicing them up like tiny sushi rolls. It's a super easy and delicious snack that kids love to make and eat!

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas, peeled
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup mini chocolate chips
  • 1/4 cup sprinkles
  • 1/4 cup shredded coconut

Steps

  1. Spread peanut butter on each banana.
  2. Roll bananas in chocolate chips, sprinkles, or coconut.
  3. Slice each banana into 1-inch "sushi" pieces.
  4. Arrange on a plate and enjoy!
