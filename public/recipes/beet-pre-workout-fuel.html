Beet Pre-Workout Fuel

Sweet+Earthy+Fresh ~90 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · energydrink

This bright pink drink is a super cool way to get ready for playing! It might sound a little funny to drink beets, but they give you lots of energy to run and jump like a superhero. It's a sweet and earthy treat that helps you power up!

Ingredients

  • 1 small cooked beet, peeled
  • 1/2 cup apple juice
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/2 inch ginger, peeled (optional)
  • 1/2 cup ice

Steps

  1. Put beet, apple juice, water, and ginger in blender.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. Add ice.
  4. Blend again until icy cold.
