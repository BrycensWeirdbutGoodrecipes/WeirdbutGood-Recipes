Mini Parm Towers

Cheesy+Tomatoey+Savory~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savoryfood

These Mini Parm Towers are like tiny lasagnas stacked up, but way easier to make! You get all the yummy cheesy, tomatoey goodness in a fun little bite-sized tower that's weirdly awesome. It's a great way to eat your veggies and cheese at the same time!

Ingredients

  • 1 medium zucchini, sliced into 1/4 inch rounds
  • 1/2 cup marinara sauce
  • 1/4 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Arrange zucchini slices on a baking sheet.
  3. Top each with sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan.
  4. Sprinkle with oregano.
  5. Bake for 10-12 minutes until cheese is bubbly.
