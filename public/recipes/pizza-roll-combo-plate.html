Pizza Roll Combo Plate

Cheesy+Crispy+Savory~750 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · junkfood

Get ready for the ultimate snack mashup! This Pizza Roll Combo Plate takes your favorite crispy pizza rolls and pairs them with other yummy treats for a super fun and easy meal. It's weird because it's a combo of all the best freezer aisle snacks, but it's good because it's delicious and quick!

Ingredients

  • 15 pizza rolls
  • 1 cup tater tots
  • 3 chicken nuggets
  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 1/4 cup ranch dressing

Steps

  1. Arrange pizza rolls, tots, and nuggets on plate.
  2. Microwave on high for 2 minutes.
  3. Stir, then microwave for 1-2 more minutes.
  4. Serve with ketchup and ranch for dipping.
