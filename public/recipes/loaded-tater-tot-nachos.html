Loaded Tater Tot Nachos

Cheesy, Crispy, Messy! +++ ~600 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · junkfood

These Loaded Tater Tot Nachos are like regular nachos, but with super crispy tater tots instead of chips! We load them up with yummy cheese, beans, and all your favorite nacho toppings for a fun, messy, and totally delicious snack or meal. It's weird, but in the best way possible!

Ingredients

  • 16 oz frozen tater tots
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup canned black beans, rinsed
  • 1/4 cup salsa
  • 2 tbsp sour cream

Steps

  1. Bake tater tots according to package.
  2. Sprinkle cheese over hot tots.
  3. Spoon beans and salsa on top.
  4. Add a dollop of sour cream.
