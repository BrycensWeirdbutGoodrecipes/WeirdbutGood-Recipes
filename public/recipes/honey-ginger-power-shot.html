Honey Ginger Power Shot

Sweet+Zingy+Spicy ~25 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · energydrink

This zingy little drink is like a superhero potion for your tummy! It's sweet from honey, a little spicy from ginger, and super refreshing to help you feel awake and ready to play. It might sound a little strange, but it tastes yummy and makes you feel like you have extra energy!

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 1/2 lemon, squeezed

Steps

  1. Pour water into a small cup.
  2. Stir in honey until dissolved.
  3. Add grated ginger and lemon juice.
  4. Mix everything together well.
  5. Drink up your power shot!
