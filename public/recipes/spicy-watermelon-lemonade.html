Spicy Watermelon Lemonade ️

Sweet+Tangy+Spicy! ~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · ogdrink

This isn't your average lemonade! Imagine juicy watermelon mixed with zesty lemon, but with a secret spicy kick that will make your taste buds tingle. It's a super fun and surprising drink that's weirdly delicious and perfect for a hot day.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups chopped watermelon
  • 1/2 cup lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • Pinch of cayenne pepper
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Blend watermelon, lemon juice, sugar, and water.
  2. Strain the mixture to remove pulp.
  3. Stir in a tiny pinch of cayenne pepper.
  4. Pour over ice and enjoy!
