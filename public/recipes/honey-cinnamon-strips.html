Honey Cinnamon Strips

Sweet+Crunchy+Cinnamon~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · sweetsnack

These Honey Cinnamon Strips are like magic toast without the toaster! You get crunchy, sweet, and cinnamony goodness all in one bite. It’s a super fun snack that tastes like a warm hug.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread
  • 1 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp sugar

Steps

  1. Cut bread into strips.
  2. Mix melted butter and honey.
  3. Brush butter mix on bread strips.
  4. Sprinkle cinnamon and sugar.
  5. Bake at 350°F for 5-7 minutes.
