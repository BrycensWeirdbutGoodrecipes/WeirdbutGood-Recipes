Matcha Grape Fizz

Sweet+Fizzy+Tangy~90 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · ogdrink

Get ready for a super cool, fizzy drink that's green like a superhero and sweet like your favorite candy! This Matcha Grape Fizz might sound a little bit wild with matcha and grape, but it's a yummy adventure for your taste buds, a sweet and tangy treat.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup grape juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon matcha powder
  • 1/4 cup sparkling water
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Mix matcha powder with a splash of hot water.
  2. Stir honey into the matcha until dissolved.
  3. Pour grape juice into a glass with ice.
  4. Add matcha mix to the glass.
  5. Top with sparkling water and stir gently.
