Volcano Burrito

Spicy+++ Cheesy+ ~350 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotfood

Get ready for a flavor explosion with the Volcano Burrito! This super fun and fiery burrito is packed with yummy ingredients that will make your taste buds tingle. It's a little bit spicy, a lot delicious, and totally unique!

Ingredients

  • 1 large tortilla
  • 1/4 cup cooked rice
  • 1/4 cup cooked ground beef
  • 2 tablespoons shredded cheese
  • 1 tablespoon hot sauce

Steps

  1. Warm tortilla in microwave.
  2. Spoon rice, beef, cheese onto tortilla.
  3. Drizzle hot sauce over ingredients.
  4. Fold tortilla into a burrito.
