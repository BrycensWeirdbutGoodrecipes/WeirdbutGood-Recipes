Ginger Lemon Zinger

Zesty+Spicy+Sweet+Refreshing~45 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · energydrink

This zippy drink is like a superhero for your tastebuds, giving you a burst of energy to play all day! It might sound a little spicy with the ginger, but the lemon makes it super yummy and refreshing. It's weird because it's so good for you, but tastes like a special treat!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, peeled
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Ask an adult to grate ginger into a mug.
  2. Squeeze lemon juice into the mug.
  3. Add honey and a pinch of salt.
  4. Pour in warm water and stir well.
  5. Enjoy your zinger!
