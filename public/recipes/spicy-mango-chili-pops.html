Spicy Mango Chili Pops ️

Sweet+Spicy+Tangy~80 cal estimate
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotsnack

Get ready for a flavor explosion with these Spicy Mango Chili Pops! They might sound a little crazy with sweet mango and a fiery kick, but trust us, it's a super fun and yummy snack. It's like a party in your mouth!

Ingredients

  • 1 large ripe mango, peeled and diced
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Blend mango, water, lime juice until smooth.
  2. Stir in chili powder and salt.
  3. Pour mixture into popsicle molds.
  4. Freeze until solid, about 4 hours.
