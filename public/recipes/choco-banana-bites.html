Choco Banana Bites

Sweet+Fruity+Crunchy~90 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · sweetsnack

Get ready for a yummy snack that's super easy to make! These Choco Banana Bites are a fun mix of sweet chocolate and creamy banana, perfect for a quick treat. It might sound a little silly, but trust us, it's so good!

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil
  • Sprinkles (optional)

Steps

  1. Slice bananas into thick coins.
  2. Melt chocolate chips with coconut oil.
  3. Dip banana slices in melted chocolate.
  4. Add sprinkles, then freeze for 15 mins.
