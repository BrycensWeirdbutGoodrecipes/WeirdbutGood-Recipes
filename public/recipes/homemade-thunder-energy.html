Homemade Thunder Energy

Sweet + Tangy + Fizzy ~80 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · ogdrink

Get ready for a delicious, bubbly drink that's like a tiny storm in a cup! It's sweet and tangy, and the fizz makes it feel super special and fun, almost like magic. This drink might seem a little unusual, but it’s a tasty adventure for your taste buds!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup grape juice
  • 1/4 cup sparkling water
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 3-4 ice cubes

Steps

  1. Pour grape juice into a glass.
  2. Add lemon juice and sugar. Stir well.
  3. Top with sparkling water.
  4. Add ice cubes. Serve immediately.
