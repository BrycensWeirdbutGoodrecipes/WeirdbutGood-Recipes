Strawberry Marshmallow Skewers

Sweet+Fruity+Squishy~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · candysnack

Get ready for a super yummy and squishy treat! We're putting sweet strawberries and fluffy marshmallows on a stick for a snack that's both fruity and melt-in-your-mouth soft. It might sound a little silly, but trust us, it's super delicious!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh strawberries, hulled
  • 1 cup mini marshmallows
  • 8 wooden skewers
  • 1 tbsp chocolate syrup (optional)

Steps

  1. Wash and dry strawberries.
  2. Carefully thread a marshmallow onto a skewer.
  3. Add a strawberry next.
  4. Alternate marshmallows and strawberries.
  5. Drizzle with chocolate syrup if you like!
