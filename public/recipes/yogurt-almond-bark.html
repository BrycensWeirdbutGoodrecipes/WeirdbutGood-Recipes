Yogurt Almond Bark

Sweet+Tangy+Crunchy ~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · energysnack

This super cool bark is like a frozen treat that's both creamy and crunchy! We use yogurt to make it tangy and sweet, then add almonds for a satisfying crunch. It's a weird-but-good snack that gives you energy to play!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup chopped almonds
  • 1 tablespoon mini chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Line a tray with parchment paper.
  2. Mix yogurt, honey, and vanilla.
  3. Spread yogurt onto paper in a thin layer.
  4. Sprinkle almonds and chocolate chips.
  5. Freeze for 2 hours until solid.
  6. Break into yummy pieces.
