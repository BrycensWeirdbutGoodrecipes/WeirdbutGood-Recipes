Mango Matcha Energy

Sweet + Fruity + Earthy ~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · energydrink

This super cool green drink is like a yummy tropical vacation in a cup, but it also gives you a boost of energy to play all day! It's a little bit sweet from the mango and a little bit earthy from the matcha, making a surprisingly delicious and vibrant combo.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup frozen mango chunks
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 teaspoon matcha powder
  • 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup water

Steps

  1. Put all ingredients in a blender.
  2. Blend until super smooth.
  3. Pour into a fun glass.
  4. Enjoy your energy boost!
