Sour Watermelon Bombs

Sour+Sweet+Fizzy~50 cal estimate
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · soursnack

Get ready for a flavor explosion with these Sour Watermelon Bombs! We're taking juicy watermelon and giving it a super sour twist that's strangely delicious. It's a fun and fizzy snack that will make your taste buds tingle!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups watermelon, cubed
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon citric acid powder
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Cut watermelon into bite-sized cubes.
  2. In a bowl, mix lime juice, sugar, salt.
  3. Add watermelon to the juice mixture.
  4. Sprinkle with citric acid powder.
  5. Toss gently to coat all the cubes.
