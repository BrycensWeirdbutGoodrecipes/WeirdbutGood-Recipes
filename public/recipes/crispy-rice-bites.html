Crispy Rice Bites

Crunchy+Savory+Garlicy~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · crunchysnack

These crispy rice bites are like a crunchy party in your mouth! We take leftover rice and make it super crispy, then add yummy flavors. It might sound a little silly, but trust us, they're super delicious and fun to eat!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (day-old is best!)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Pinch of black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add rice, spread it out flat.
  3. Sprinkle with garlic powder, salt, pepper.
  4. Cook until golden and crispy, stirring.
  5. Serve warm and enjoy the crunch!
