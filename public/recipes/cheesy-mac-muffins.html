Cheesy Mac Muffins

Cheesy++ Crispy+ ~250 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · savoryfood

These Cheesy Mac Muffins are like your favorite macaroni and cheese, but in a fun, grab-and-go muffin shape! They're super yummy and easy to eat, perfect for little hands. You'll love how crispy they get on the outside and gooey on the inside!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked macaroni
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • Pinch of salt
  • Pinch of pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Mix all ingredients in a bowl.
  3. Spoon into greased muffin tin.
  4. Bake for 15-20 minutes.
