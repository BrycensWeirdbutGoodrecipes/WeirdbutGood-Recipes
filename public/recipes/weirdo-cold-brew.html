Weirdo Cold Brew

Sweet + Chocolate + Yum! ~180 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · ogdrink

This is not your grown-up's coffee! It's a super cool, super sweet, and super chocolatey cold brew drink that's a little weird but a whole lot yummy. It's like a special treat that wakes up your taste buds with a happy dance!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cold brew coffee (decaf for kids!)
  • 1/4 cup chocolate syrup
  • 2 tablespoons sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup whole milk
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Pour cold brew into a glass.
  2. Add chocolate syrup and condensed milk.
  3. Stir well to mix everything.
  4. Add vanilla and regular milk.
  5. Fill glass with ice cubes.
  6. Stir one last time and enjoy!
