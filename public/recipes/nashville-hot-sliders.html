Nashville Hot Sliders

Spicy +++ Tangy ~300 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · hotfood

These tiny chicken sandwiches pack a big, spicy punch that's super fun to eat! They might make your tongue tingle, but the cool pickles and soft buns make the hot chicken a crazy delicious combo you won't forget. It's like a flavor adventure in every bite!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked chicken, shredded
  • 4 mini slider buns
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • 1 tbsp butter, melted
  • 4 pickle slices
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper

Steps

  1. Mix chicken, hot sauce, butter, and cayenne.
  2. Spoon chicken mix onto slider buns.
  3. Top each with a pickle slice.
  4. Serve immediately and enjoy!
