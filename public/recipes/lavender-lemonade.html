Lavender Lemonade

Floral + Sweet + Tangy ~150 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · mocktaildrink

Get ready for a magical drink that's both flowery and zingy! This lavender lemonade is a super cool purple color and tastes like a sunny garden party in a cup. It might sound a little strange, but it's a yummy surprise your taste buds will love!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tsp dried lavender
  • 1 cup lemon juice
  • 3 cups cold water
  • Lemon slices for garnish

Steps

  1. Boil sugar and 1 cup water to make syrup.
  2. Add lavender to hot syrup; steep for 10 mins.
  3. Strain out lavender from the syrup.
  4. Mix syrup, lemon juice, and cold water.
  5. Serve over ice with a lemon slice.
