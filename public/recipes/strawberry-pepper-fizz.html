Strawberry Pepper Fizz

Sweet+Berry+Spicy~90 cal
By Brycen Perina (age 11) · fruitdrink

This yummy pink drink is super fizzy and tastes like strawberries, but with a secret kick! We add a tiny bit of pepper to make it extra interesting and tingly on your tongue. It's a fun and surprising treat!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sliced strawberries
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 cup lemon-lime soda
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Mash strawberries, pepper, and sugar.
  2. Stir in water.
  3. Pour mixture into a glass with ice.
  4. Top with lemon-lime soda.
