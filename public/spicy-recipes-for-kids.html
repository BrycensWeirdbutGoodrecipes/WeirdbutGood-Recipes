How to handle the heat

If something's too spicy, drink milk, not water. Water spreads capsaicin (the spicy chemical) around your mouth. Milk has casein, which sticks to capsaicin and washes it away. Yogurt works too. Ice cream is even better. Bread also helps soak it up.

And honestly — start slow. Ghost peppers are no joke. Try the Spicy Mango Pops first, then work your way up.

Got a friend who claims they can handle heat? Tell them to try the Ghost Pepper Wings and watch them suffer 😂
