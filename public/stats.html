🎯 What this shows

Total Visits — every time someone (including you) loads the site.
Members — people who signed in with an email.
Recipes Printed — anyone who hit Print or asked you to ship one.
Tips Received — total $ from donations.
Custom Recipes — recipes users made with AI ("Make Your Own").
Total Actions — every clicky thing logged across the site.
