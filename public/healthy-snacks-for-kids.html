What makes a snack actually healthy?

Real talk: "healthy" is more than calories. I look for snacks that have:

• Real food ingredients (fruit, veggies, cheese, yogurt, nuts) — nothing you can't pronounce
• Some protein OR fiber — so you actually feel full instead of hungry again in 20 min
• Low or no added sugar — fruit sugar = fine, candy sugar = not
• Actually taste good — otherwise I'm just going to eat a pop-tart anyway

If you're a parent reading this: kids will eat healthy food if it looks cool and tastes good. The boring carrot stick approach doesn't work. Try the Watermelon Feta Bites — they look impressive and only have like 5 ingredients.
