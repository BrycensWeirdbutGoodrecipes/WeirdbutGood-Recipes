Best Mocktails for Kids 🥂

By Brycen Perina (age 11) · June 11, 2026 · 3 min read

A mocktail is just a fancy drink without alcohol. Adults make them for parties so the people who don't drink still get something cool. But honestly? Kids deserve fancy drinks too.

These are 8 of my favorite fancy-pants drinks. Perfect for birthdays, sleepovers, or those nights when your parents are having a dinner party and you don't want to drink boring water out of a regular cup.

How to make any mocktail look fancy

Trick #1: Use a real glass, not a plastic cup. Glass changes the vibe by like 200%.

Trick #2: Add a garnish — a sprig of mint, a slice of cucumber, a lime wedge, a few berries. Whatever matches the drink. Free upgrade to look like a restaurant drink.

Trick #3: Use a coupe glass or a martini glass if your parents have them. The shape itself makes it feel fancy.

Trick #4: Rim the glass — wet the edge with lime juice, dip in sugar or salt. Boom, looks pro.

Try the Hibiscus Lime Cooler first if you want a wow moment. The color alone is worth it.
