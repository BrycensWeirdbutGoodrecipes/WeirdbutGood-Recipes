Best Energy Drinks for Kids ⚡

By Brycen Perina (age 11) · June 11, 2026 · 3 min read

I'm 11 years old and I can't drink Monster or Red Bull or any of that stuff. My mom says I'm "already wired enough." But I still like the IDEA of an energy drink — something cold, fizzy, with flavor that makes you feel like you're about to do something cool.

So I made my own. These are real recipes, no caffeine pills, no weird chemicals. Just real ingredients you have at home that give you actual energy (mostly from natural sugar + electrolytes + sometimes a tiny bit of caffeine from matcha or espresso).

⚠️ Heads up to parents: Some of these use matcha or coffee. Use the kid-version (decaf or skip it) if your kid is younger than 10 or sensitive to caffeine.

1Mango Matcha Energy

This is my #1 favorite. Matcha gives you slow energy (way better than Red Bull crash), mango makes it actually taste good. Ice cold = like a tropical Starbucks. Full recipe →

2Ginger Lemon Zinger

No caffeine in this one. Ginger gives you a kick that wakes you up without making you jittery. Lemon = vitamin C. Cold + zingy. Good after sports practice. Full recipe →

3Tropical Storm Boost

Coconut water for electrolytes (way better than Gatorade because no weird coloring), pineapple, lime, and a splash of orange juice. Tastes like a beach day. Full recipe →

5Yerba Mate Citrus Bomb

6Honey Ginger Power Shot

7Coconut Lime Electrolyte

8Homemade Thunder Energy

Why these are better than store energy drinks

Real talk: Red Bull and Monster have like 200mg of caffeine per can. That's WAY too much for a kid (or even most adults). They also have a bunch of food dyes and stuff your body doesn't actually need. My recipes use real foods that give you energy the way nature meant — slowly and steadily.

Also they cost less. A 4-pack of Monster is like $10. I can make a glass of Mango Matcha Energy for about $1.50.

If you make one and like it, share it with a friend or come say hi. Try the Mango Matcha Energy first if you don't know where to start. It's the gateway drug.
