About this site 🦊

Hey, I'm Brycen. I'm 11 years old, I live in Staten Island, NY, and I made this whole site myself with help from my dad and our AI fox Kit.

I started cooking weird stuff because regular kid recipes are boring. Why drink plain lemonade when you could try a Pickle Brine Sparkler or a Ghost Pepper Cold Brew? Some of these recipes are actually weird-but-good. A few are just weird. That's the whole point.

Every recipe card on this site is hand-drawn by AI in a style I picked out — ingredients on the left, steps on the right, doodles everywhere. There are over 150 recipes split across drinks (coffee, energy, fruit, hot, mocktails, low-cal, savory), foods (full meals — handhelds, bowls, wraps, sliders), and snacks (quick bites + treats). Includes pescetarian and vegetarian options.

My dad runs 3dmikep.com (3D printing, automation, AI consulting) and 2dmikep.com (his book "Reprogramming Reality" + 2D design work). He taught me a lot of what I know about building stuff. Go check him out.

If you make one of my recipes, leave a tip or just share the link with a friend. I built the whole thing — frontend, AI image generation, photo-to-recipe upload, friend cook-off feature, donations — all of it. If you're a kid reading this thinking "I could do that too" — you can. Ask an adult for help, start small, build something weird.

Recipes I'm most proud of: Dad's Dragon Fuel 🐉, Bacon Mac Bombs 🥓, Frozen Grape Bombs 🍇, Espresso Date Bites ☕, Power Trail Mix Bars ⚡, Matcha Coconut Latte, Pizza Pocket Bombs 💣, Cinnamon Maple Cold Foam Latte, Mocha Mint Frappé, Fire Honey Tenders 🔥, Green Thunder Energy Latte 🍵, Watermelon Feta Bites 🍉.
