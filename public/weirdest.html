Top 10 Weirdest Recipes for Kids 🦊

By Brycen Perina (age 11) · June 10, 2026 · 4 min read

I'm 11 and I made 100 recipes. Some are normal. Some are weird. Some are weird-but-good — which is the entire point of my site. Here are the 10 weirdest things I actually made, taste-tested, and decided were worth keeping.

1Pickle Brine Sparkler

Hear me out. Pickle juice + sparkling water + lime + ice. It sounds wrong but it tastes like a salty lemonade and it's actually really refreshing on a hot day. My friends thought I was joking. They tried it. They asked for seconds. See the full recipe →

2Ghost Pepper Wings

I love hot stuff. Ghost peppers are about 100× hotter than jalapeños. I made wings with a ghost pepper honey glaze. My older brother cried. I ate four. Full recipe →

3Frozen Grape Bombs

Just frozen grapes. That's the whole thing. But they taste like little sorbet candy and they're 0 effort. Sometimes the weirdest recipes are the simplest. Full recipe →

4Cucumber Hummus Boats

Cucumber cut into boats, scooped out, filled with hummus, topped with paprika and dill. Sounds healthy. Tastes amazing. Low-calorie which my mom likes, weird-looking which my friends like. Full recipe →

5Cinnamon Maple Cold Foam Latte

This is my favorite drink I made. Espresso + oat milk + maple syrup + cinnamon + a layer of cold foam on top. Better than Starbucks. I'm not kidding. Full recipe →

6Watermelon Feta Bites

Watermelon + feta cheese + a little balsamic. The combo sounds wrong but the salty-sweet thing is what makes restaurants charge $14 for it. I made it at home for like $2. Full recipe →

7Tropical Crunch Bowl

Pineapple + mango + granola + coconut + a drizzle of honey. Eat with a spoon. Tastes like vacation. Full recipe →

8Espresso Date Bites

Dates + ground espresso + almond butter + sea salt, rolled into balls. They taste like little coffee energy bombs. Don't eat them at 9pm unless you want to be awake at 1am. Full recipe →

9Sriracha Pretzel Bites

Soft pretzel bites tossed in butter, sriracha, and a little honey. Spicy + salty + sweet at the same time. My new favorite snack. Full recipe →

10Strawberry Avocado Toast

Avocado toast is a meme but adding sliced strawberries + balsamic + flaky salt actually makes it incredible. The strawberries + creamy avocado is unexpected. Trust me. Full recipe →

Why I made this site

I made this site because regular kid recipe sites are boring. Sliders. Pizza. Plain smoothies. Yawn. Real kids try weird stuff in the kitchen and most of it actually works.

Every recipe on weirdbutgood.recipes is hand-drawn by AI in a style I designed, with ingredients on the left and steps on the right. I built the site myself with help from my dad (who runs a 3D printing company) and our AI assistant Kit (who I gave a fox personality).

If you make one of these and like it, share the link with a friend. That's how the site grows.

🐉 Special spotlight: Dad's Dragon Fuel

My dad makes the weirdest drinks. There's one he calls Dad's Dragon Fuel — lemon-lime soda, pineapple juice, lime juice, vanilla ice cream, and three drops of green food coloring so it looks like swamp water. It tastes way better than it looks. He drinks it when he's working late and says it "fuels the dragon." When I made it once it fizzed all over the counter.
