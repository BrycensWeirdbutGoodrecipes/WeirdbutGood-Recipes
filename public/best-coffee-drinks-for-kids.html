Can kids really drink coffee?

Short answer: depends on your kid + how much. Most pediatricians say under 12, no caffeine, including soda. Over 12, maybe like 100mg max per day (one small cup). I use decaf or matcha when I want the flavor without the buzz.

For really little kids: just use decaf espresso or skip the coffee shot entirely. The recipes still taste amazing without it because the actual flavor comes from the syrups + milk + foam, not just the coffee.

Want to start somewhere? Try the Cinnamon Maple Cold Foam Latte. It's the best one. I promise.
